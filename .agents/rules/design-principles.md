# Design & Content Standards

## Core Principles
1. **Zero AI Aesthetic**: No sparkles (`✨`), emojis, decorative unicode symbols, neon glows, or blurry gradient fades.
2. **Grounded Human Copy**: No inflated buzzword titles or artificial corporate jargon. Direct, honest developer descriptions only.
3. **Institutional UI Quality**: Crisp 1px solid borders, solid surface colors, consistent border radii, and restrained color accents.
