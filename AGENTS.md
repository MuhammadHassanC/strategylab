# Project Guidelines & Design Standards

## UI / UX & Content Rules
- **No AI Aesthetic Clichés**: Avoid sparkles (`✨`), decorative emojis, neon glows, fuzzy gradient fades, or pill badges with artificial shadows.
- **No Fluff or AI Titles**: Do not invent buzzword-bloated corporate titles. Write genuine, grounded developer copy.
- **Human Fintech Minimalist**: High contrast, crisp 1px borders, consistent sizing, and restrained, intentional color accents.
